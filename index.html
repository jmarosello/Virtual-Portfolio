Selected work

AI-assisted listing workflow

  • Helium 10
  • Data Dive
  • Claude
The problem
Writing one complete listing by hand took about two hours.
What I built
A Claude workflow that turns Helium 10 keyword exports into a full listing: keyword table, competitor review insights, title, five bullets, description, A+ headlines and alt text, backend search terms, and image overlay copy.
Result
About 35 minutes per listing, including my own brand-voice review. It can draft five listings in one pass.
